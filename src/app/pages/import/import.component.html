TuneMyMusic

Recommended

Free. Exports playlists from Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer or YouTube Music to a file.

Playlists from other users are limited to 500 songs. To leave them out, unselect them in TuneMyMusic.
  1. 1
    Open TuneMyMusic
  2. 2
    Choose your streaming service as the source, log in, and load your library.
  3. 3
    Unselect the playlists you don't want, then choose Export to file, CSV.
  4. 4
    Save the file in your Downloads folder. It is imported automatically.

Spotify data export

Free. Spotify takes up to 30 days to prepare it.

  1. 1
    Open Spotify privacy settings
  2. 2
    Under Download your data, check Account data and request it.
  3. 3
    When Spotify emails you, download the zip file to your Downloads folder. It is imported automatically.
