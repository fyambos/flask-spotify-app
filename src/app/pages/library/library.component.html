On your devices

Mac and iPhone

Update Apple Music adds the songs and writes your playlists in a “Music Sorter” folder. Run it after each sync.

In Music → Settings → Files, turn off “Copy files to Music Media folder”.

Sync the iPhone from Finder, or with Sync Library if you have Apple Music or iTunes Match.

Android

Copy the music folder to the phone, or keep it in sync with Syncthing. Poweramp, Samsung Music and VLC read the playlists.

Windows

Open the music folder in MusicBee, foobar2000 or VLC. The playlists are in the Playlists folder.
