On your devices

Mac and iPhone

Apple Music gets the songs and your playlists, in a “Music Sorter” folder.

iPhone, set up once:

With an Apple Music or iTunes Match subscription, turn on Sync Library on the Mac and the iPhone. New songs arrive by themselves.

Without it, in Finder select the iPhone, sync the “Music Sorter” playlists and turn on “Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi”. It syncs while the iPhone charges on the same Wi-Fi.

Windows and iPhone

iTunes gets the songs and your playlists, in a “Music Sorter” folder.

Set up once:

  1. Install iTunes from the Microsoft Store and open it once. Don't install Apple Music or Apple Devices, iTunes would lose the music.
  2. Connect the iPhone, select it in iTunes, sync the “Music Sorter” playlists and turn on “Sync with this iPhone over Wi-Fi”.
Android

Set up once:

  1. Install Syncthing on the computer (syncthing.net) and Syncthing-Fork on the phone (from F-Droid or Google Play).
  2. Pair them, then share the folder {{ state?.music_dir }}.
  3. In Poweramp, add the folder. It reads the playlists.

The folder syncs when the phone and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi.
